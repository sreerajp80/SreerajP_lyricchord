# Plan: Fix Chord Color Visibility in Dark and Light Themes

**Status:** completed

## Issue
In both Light and Dark themes, chords rendered on the song sheet (`ChordSheetView`) are barely visible or completely unreadable:
- **Light Theme**: `_lightScheme.secondary` was set to `AppColors.lightSecondaryContainer` (`0xFFEADDFF` - faint light purple). Rendered against `AppColors.lightBg` (`0xFFFDF8FD`), the contrast ratio is ~1.1:1 (light text on light background).
- **Dark Theme**: `_darkScheme.secondary` was set to `AppColors.darkSecondaryContainer` (`0xFF4F378B` - dark violet). Rendered against `AppColors.darkBg` (`0xFF141218`), the contrast ratio is ~2.1:1 (dark text on dark background).

## Fix Strategy
1. **Fix M3 ColorScheme mapping in `lib/theme/app_theme.dart`**:
   - Change `_lightScheme.secondary` from `lightSecondaryContainer` (`0xFFEADDFF`) to `AppColors.lightPrimary` (`0xFF6750A4`), providing ~7.1:1 contrast on light backgrounds.
   - Change `_darkScheme.secondary` from `darkSecondaryContainer` (`0xFF4F378B`) to `AppColors.darkPrimary` (`0xFFD0BCFF`), providing ~10.5:1 contrast on dark backgrounds.
2. **Update Chord Styling in `lib/widgets/chord_sheet_view.dart`**:
   - Set `chordStyle.color` to `theme.colorScheme.primary` so chord badges stand out cleanly over lyrics, matching the primary accent color used in `GuitarChordDiagram` and `HarmoniumChordDiagram`.
3. **Add Widget/Theme Tests**:
   - Create tests in `test/widgets/chord_sheet_view_test.dart` to verify chord text colors in both light and dark mode, confirming proper visibility and high contrast.
