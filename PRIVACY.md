# Privacy Policy — LyricChord

> **Draft.** This text describes what the app does today. Before any store release it must be
> published at a public URL and that URL recorded in `docs/PROJECT_PROFILE.md`.

**Last updated:** 2026-10-23

---

## 1. Summary

LyricChord does not collect, send or share any personal data. It has no network access at all.
Everything you create stays on your device.

## 2. What the app stores

- Your songs, setlists and notes, in a database on your device.
- Your settings (theme, fonts, scroll speed and similar), on your device.

The app never sends this data anywhere. On Android it is left out of the system backup
(`allowBackup` is off). On iOS, your own device backup (iCloud or computer) can include it, as
with other app data. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the device, and so does
**Settings → Delete all data** inside the app.

## 3. What the app does not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising.
- No internet connection — the app has no network permission.

## 4. Device features the app uses

Each feature is used only when you turn it on, and its data never leaves the device:

| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| Camera | To scan an AirQR code when you choose to receive a song from another device. Camera images are not saved. |
| Photo picker | To read one photo you pick, only to find an AirQR code in it. |
| Fingerprint / Face ID | To unlock Stage Lock. The check is done by the system; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. |
| Do Not Disturb access (Android) | So Stage Quiet Mode can silence the ringer while you perform. |

## 5. Files you import or export

Import and export use the system file picker and share sheet. The app reads only the file you
pick, and shares a file only to the app you choose.

## 6. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
