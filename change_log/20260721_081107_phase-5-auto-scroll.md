# Change log — Phase 5: Auto-scroll

**Date:** 2026-07-21

Implements [../plans/20260721_075947_phase-5-auto-scroll.md](../plans/20260721_075947_phase-5-auto-scroll.md)
(Phase 5 of the implementation plan): hands-free scrolling of the chord sheet in
the performance view, in two modes.

---

## What changed

### New files

- **`lib/services/auto_scroll_calculator.dart`** — a pure service (no Flutter
  imports) with all the timing math:
  - `tempoDuration(bpm, measures, {beatsPerMeasure = 4})` → song length.
  - `tempoPixelsPerSecond(contentExtent, duration)` → the speed that covers the
    whole sheet in exactly the computed time (this is what makes Tempo Auto end
    at song end).
  - `manualPixelsPerSecond(speedMultiplier)` → base speed scaled by 0.5/1/2.

- **`lib/providers/auto_scroll_provider.dart`** — `AutoScrollSettings`
  (immutable view state: mode, speed, measures, isPlaying, contentExtent),
  `AutoScrollMode`, and `AutoScrollNotifier` with `setMode`, `setSpeed`,
  `increaseMeasures`/`decreaseMeasures`, `play`/`pause`/`togglePlay`/`stop`,
  `reportContentExtent`, and `resetAll`.

- **`lib/widgets/auto_scroll_controls.dart`** — the auto-scroll UI: a
  Manual/Tempo segmented toggle, a play/pause button, the 0.5x/1x/2x chips
  (Manual) or a measures stepper with a live duration + px/second read-out
  (Tempo).

### Changed files

- **`lib/widgets/performance_controls.dart`** — now takes the song's `bpm` and
  adds `AutoScrollControls` as the last section inside the controls card.

- **`lib/screens/song_performance_screen.dart`** — `_SongPage` is now a
  `ConsumerStatefulWidget` with `SingleTickerProviderStateMixin`. It owns the
  `ScrollController` and a `Ticker`. While playing it advances the scroll offset
  each frame at the calculator's speed, reports its scroll length so the Tempo
  read-out is accurate, and stops itself at the bottom. Only the on-screen page
  scrolls (pre-built pager neighbours stay put). Swiping to another song now
  also resets the auto-scroll state.

### Tests

- **`test/services/auto_scroll_calculator_test.dart`** — duration and px/second
  math, including the "ends exactly at song end" check (px/s × seconds equals
  the full content length), and edge/zero cases.
- **`test/providers/auto_scroll_provider_test.dart`** — mode/speed/measures
  transitions, clamping, play/pause, extent reporting, and `resetAll`.
- **`test/screens/song_performance_screen_test.dart`** — the auto-scroll section
  renders, Tempo mode shows the measures stepper, and tapping Play flips the
  playing state (tested on the controls in isolation to avoid running the
  ticker in a widget test).
- Deleted **`test/screens/scratch_debug_test.dart`** (leftover scratch/debug
  file, confirmed in the plan).
