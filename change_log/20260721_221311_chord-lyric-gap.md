# Reduce the gap between chords and lyrics

**Plan:** plans/20260721_221311_chord-lyric-gap.md

## What changed

`lib/widgets/chord_sheet_view.dart` only:

1. The chord slot above each lyric chunk was shrunk from
   `chordStyle.fontSize! * 1.4` to `* 1.1`, so the box is exactly as
   tall as the chord text and no dead space is left below the chord.
2. The lyric line height was reduced from `1.35` to `1.2`, trimming the
   extra leading above the lyric line.

Together these bring the chord symbols much closer to their lyric line,
matching the original Android app.

## Checks

- `flutter analyze` — no issues.
- `flutter test` — all 174 tests passed.

## Not done (out of scope)

The Android app draws chords as small filled badges; the Flutter port
